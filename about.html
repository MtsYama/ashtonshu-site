Ashton Z. Shu

Also known as Zhengshen Shu.

I’m a designer and a community builder exploring how AI and human creativity can learn from each other.

My path into design wasn’t linear — I started in electrical engineering and later worked in hospital operations, where I learned how systems, people, and details connect. That mindset still shapes how I think about design today.

Now, I’m growing as a graphic designer in Canada and helping build Superlinear Academy, a creative AI community that encourages people not just to use AI tools, but to think and create with them. We focus on building habits and mindsets that strengthen both logic and intuition.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to things that explore feeling and form — from typography and visual storytelling to sensory design, mixology, and even Tarot.

I believe good design, like good conversation, helps people see things differently. It also applies to AI — the more thoughtful the dialogue, the more meaningful the outcome.
