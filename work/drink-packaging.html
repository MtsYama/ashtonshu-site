Drink Packaging

Package Design For A Drink

student project

A packaging redesign for Faygo 60/40, a grapefruit and lime soda, with bottle labels and a four-bottle carrier designed to create a cohesive retail presence.

The brief called for a carrier that holds four 300 mL bottles, plus the bottle’s body label, neck label and cap, using the brand’s own logo and copy, a UPC code, a nutrition table and the information Canada requires on packaging.

Carrier

Digital mock-ups built from photographs of the folded prototype. The carrier’s copy runs in English and French, with a bilingual nutrition table.

Bottle

The body and neck labels on a 300 mL glass bottle. The body label wraps around, with the 60/40 logo on the front and the nutrition table at the back.

Flat Artwork

The flat artwork at actual size: the carrier with its die lines, folds and glue areas marked, beside the body label, the neck label and the cap.

Process

The first digital draft set both faces of the carrier in English; in the final artwork one face is in French.

Plain white prototypes, cut and folded from the die line, tested the handle and the divider that keeps four bottles apart.
