Campaign Works

Campaign Designs

selections

A collection of campaign visuals created for different contexts, including restaurant promotion, social media marketing, and event posters.

Jak’s Diner

A student campaign for Jak’s Diner in Sudbury, built on the line Make Morning Easy: an ad for a bus-stop bench, two Facebook stories for the menu and the opening hours, and a mailer with the address.

Beef Wellington

A recipe illustration for a class brief, set as a limited-time Chef’s Special for The Keg Steakhouse + Bar. Each ingredient is drawn on its own around four slices of the finished dish, and the illustrations carry over to a postcard and a T-shirt, shown here with the poster in a street mockup.

IKEA

Three digital ads for IKEA under the line The Wonderful Everyday, one for each product range: the Oskarshamn armchair and footstool, Skådis pegboards and accessories, and the Höstagille fall and Halloween décor. Each product is drawn in flat blue on IKEA yellow, with three colour swatches.

Book Launch

A launch poster and a bookmark for my typography book, Play with Every Letter, a typographic journey through global pangrams.

Workshop Posters

Posters for screen printing, riso printing and photography workshops, each set up for riso printing in two inks: Metallic Gold with Kelly Green, Federal Blue or Fluorescent Orange. The photographs are mine.

Annie Atkins Talk

A poster, an invitation card and a booklet for an RGD talk by Annie Atkins, a graphic designer for film, under the title Faded Colors. The brief set the date, the venue at Cambrian College’s Open Studio and the evening’s agenda; the booklet adds her biography.
