Tarot Deck

Tarot Deck Illustration & Print Design

student project

A tarot deck exploring the emotional journey of a single character through symbolic visual storytelling.

Each card represents a moment in her life, expressed through minimal geometric illustration and textured print aesthetics.

Print

The print file sets eight cards on an 11 by 17 inch sheet: eight backs on the first page, and the four fronts, two of each, on the second.

Process

Made for Assignment 2 of Illustration I at Cambrian College, titled Saul Bass Card. The cards began as pencil sketches on grid paper. A first digital set in flat colour, numbered VI, I, VII and IV, came before the final cards with their printed texture and names.

Test prints: a full proof sheet with a trimmed card on top, and four cards cut out with rounded corners.
